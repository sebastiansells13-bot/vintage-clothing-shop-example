title: How We Grade Condition
description: What "Excellent," "Very Good," and "Good" actually mean on our listings.
date: 2026-02-15
tags: shop-notes
---
Vintage condition grading is inconsistent across the internet, so here's exactly what
we mean:

**Excellent** — no visible flaws, could pass for new. **Very Good** — small,
honest signs of wear (light fading, a tiny mark) that don't affect wearability.
**Good** — wearable and structurally sound, but with a flaw worth knowing about
before you buy (noted in the listing, not hidden).

We don't list anything with holes, stains that didn't come out, or damage we
wouldn't want to discover after paying for it ourselves.
